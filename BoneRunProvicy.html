Bone Run · Android game

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

Bone Run (Android package com.authorityx.bonerun) is provided by Craig Griffiths. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Bone Run is an offline game. It does not collect or transmit personal information, require an account, or include advertising or analytics services.

Information accessed, collected and shared

Bone Run processes your gameplay inputs on your device to operate the game. It does not send these inputs or gameplay activity to us or to third parties. The game does not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or other personal information.

We do not sell, share or use player information for advertising or tracking. The game does not include an online account system, third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

Local device storage

The game may create local application files necessary for operation. Its development preview function can save a gameplay image to the game's private application storage. These files remain on your device and are not uploaded by the game.

Permissions and security

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location. The current Android release does not request Internet access. Local application files are handled through Android's application storage protections. We do not maintain a server containing player data.

Retention and deletion

Because the game does not transmit personal information to us, we do not retain personal information about players on our servers. Local application files remain until removed by the game, by clearing the game's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game. There is no game account to delete.

Children's privacy

The game's data practices apply to players of all ages. Bone Run does not knowingly collect personal information from children and does not provide accounts, advertising or tracking.

Google Play and device services

Google Play and Android may process information independently when you download, purchase or manage apps or use device services. Their processing is governed by their own policies, including Google's Privacy Policy. This is separate from information collected by the game.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the game's information practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Contact
